Windows are 1, 2, 3 or 6 hours, with endpoints within 5 minutes. Starts must lie within the selected {{ analysis.input.storm_proximity_nm }} nm proximity; endpoints may leave it. Reject observation gaps over 30 minutes, conflicting timestamps and position jumps implying 50 kt or more. Storm observations must have tropical/subtropical storm or hurricane status and intensity of at least 34 kt.

Towards/away requires a valid starting speed above 2 and below 50 kt and starting course, an ending speed from 0 to below 50 kt, displacement above 2 kt, and at least 5 nm vessel contribution in either direction. A vessel may stop at the end of a movement window. Stationary requires all reported speeds from 0 to 1 kt and all positions within 1 nm of the start. Net changes below 5 nm do not establish a direction.

The scoring policy remains limited to starts 50–70 nm away. Increasing actual separation with at least 5 nm vessel benefit and at least 3 nm storm closure earns a 5-point credit. Stationary approach of at least 5 nm adds 5 points per actual hour, capped at 20. Other patterns receive no automatic adjustment, including movement towards the storm. For each storm/pattern, retain the largest applicable adjustment, then strongest geometry; overlapping windows are not summed within a pattern.
