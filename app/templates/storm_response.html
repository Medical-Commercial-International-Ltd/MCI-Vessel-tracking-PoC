Exact movement calculations and thresholds

Distances use the haversine formula with Earth radius 3,440.065 nautical miles. Start/end separation = distance(vessel position, simultaneous HURDAT2 storm position). Staying-put separation = distance(start vessel position, end storm position). Movement benefit = actual end separation − staying-put separation.

Compare original observations over 1, 2, 3 and 6 hours, using the nearest endpoint within 5 minutes. Reject any window with an observation gap above 30 minutes. Start/end storm separation is calculated at the preceding whole minute; the staying-put calculation uses the exact endpoint timestamp. Tropical/subtropical storm or hurricane status and at least 34 kt intensity are required.

Moved away: starts 50–70 nm away (subject to selected proximity), valid starting speed above 2 and below 50 kt and course below 360°, storm would close at least 3 nm if the vessel stayed, movement benefit at least 5 nm, and actual separation increases. Underway as storm approached: separation falls at least 5 nm, valid speeds at both endpoints, endpoint courses differ by no more than 15°, and position displacement implies above 2 kt. Endpoint courses do not establish an unchanged route throughout the window.

Stationary approach: every reported speed is valid and at most 1 kt, every observed position remains within 1 nm of the starting position, and separation falls at least 5 nm. Harbour or anchorage protection must be reviewed. Each storm retains its strongest example of each pattern; overlapping windows are not added together.

Weather scoring: stationary approach adds 5 points per hour in the strongest verified window, capped at 20. A verified movement-away window reduces the encounter by 5 points. The existing proximity, wind-field, intensity and duration components remain; event scores are bounded to 0–100. No original broadcasts means no movement adjustment.
