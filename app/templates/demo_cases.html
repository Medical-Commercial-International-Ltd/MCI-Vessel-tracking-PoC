Decision rules

DEMO-APPETITE-v2 is a proposed risk appetite for this demonstration. Scores remain unchanged. These rules recommend an action; the underwriter makes the decision.

  1. Historical loss cases: do not offer for an equivalent submission with the investigation's unsafe navigation conditions unresolved. The assessment identifies the post-event source; this is retrospective review, not a pre-loss prediction.
  2. Do not offer terms: verified expiry or rejection of medical-fitness or role-competency evidence for an assigned crew member. Reconsider after an acceptable replacement and role suitability review.
  3. Do not offer terms: weather exposure of 80/100 or above, using the current model with medium or high tracking confidence. Reconsider a materially revised cruising and storm plan. Observed stationary approach is referred for shelter review instead.
  4. Refer: low tracking confidence, uncertain identity, missing or unverified crew evidence, movement towards a storm, stationary approach, or weather / combined scores of 60/100 or above that do not meet a decline rule.
  5. Offer terms subject to checks: no unresolved blocker or referral. Confirm survey, claims, value, cruising area, full manning, certificate suitability and storm plan. Arrange renewals for certificates expiring soon.

80 marks the existing severe band; 60 marks the high band. They are proposed appetite boundaries, not validated probabilities of loss. A confirmed blocker takes precedence over missing evidence; a low score never overrides missing evidence.

Bounty · Sandy · 29 October 2012

The closest match to this demonstration

The NTSB attributed the sinking to the captain's decision to sail into Sandy's forecast path. It identified an ageing vessel, an inexperienced crew and ineffective organisational oversight.

Proposed decision: do not offer with these conditions unresolved. Review vessel condition, heavy-weather competence and an independently reviewed passage and storm plan. This is a retrospective scenario, not a finding that an insurer actually declined cover.

El Faro · Joaquin · 1 October 2015

Navigation and safety management

The NTSB found insufficient action to avoid Joaquin, failure to use current weather information and ineffective bridge teamwork. The cargo ship sank with all 33 people aboard lost.

Proposed decision: do not offer with these conditions unresolved. Require evidence of effective forecast use, escalation, bridge teamwork and shoreside oversight. This is a commercial cargo reference. Enter MMSI 368208000 or select El Faro in Demo yachts to run the recorded voyage.
