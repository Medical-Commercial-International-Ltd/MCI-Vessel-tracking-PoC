How crew affects the assessment

Higher scores mean larger evidence gaps. Each role uses provisional safety and medical evidence slots, plus competency evidence for Captain, Chief officer and Engineer. Competency has weight 3, medical weight 2 and each of four safety slots weight 1. These are POC review rules, not statutory certification requirements.

For each document we take the highest applicable penalty: rejected or expired 100; referred/queries or expiry before assignment end 75; missing/unrecognised verification 60; pending verification 50; reviewed/checked or unreviewed details 40; expiry within 90 days 35; missing expiry 25; missing issue date 15. Missing required evidence scores 100. Verified status alone scores 0; date and review checks still apply. Conflicting checks use the worst result; duplicate documents use the lowest gap score for that evidence slot.

Each person's score is the weighted mean of their slots; the roster uses its highest individual score. Combined = cyclone exposure + round((100 − cyclone exposure) × 0.25 × crew score ÷ 100). This adds up to 25 points and never reduces exposure. Missing, rejected, expired, referred or assignment-period expiry evidence triggers review; so does a missing Captain or a score of 60 or above. Unknown or stale profiles leave the combined score incomplete.
